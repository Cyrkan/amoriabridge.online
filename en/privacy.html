Privacy Policy

The administration of the AmoriaBridge website is committed to protecting your privacy online by ensuring a high level of data security. This Privacy Policy is based on the requirements of the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) of the European Union. The collection of personal data is aimed at improving the functionality of our service, maintaining communication with website visitors, providing users with the requested information, and fulfilling other defined purposes.

Collection and Use of Personal Data

We collect personal data only with your explicit consent. This may include your first name, last name, and phone number. The processing of this data is carried out in accordance with the applicable legislation of the European Union and Ukraine.

Data Storage, Modification, and Deletion

A user who has provided personal data to AmoriaBridge has the full right to edit and delete it, as well as to withdraw consent for its use. Personal data is stored for a period of 6 months. The website administration deletes the data after its use is completed. To access personal data, users may contact us at info@amoriabridge.Personal data may be transferred to third parties only with the user’s explicit consent.

Use of Technical Data When Visiting the Website

When visiting AmoriaBridge, technical data such as IP address, visit time, browser settings, and operating system may be stored in the database. This data cannot be used to identify a specific individual.

Information Provided by Children

Parents or legal guardians who discover that their children have provided personal data without consent may contact us at info@amoriabridge. Minors are prohibited from providing personal data on the website without the consent of a parent or legal guardian.

Use of Cookies

AmoriaBridge uses cookies to ensure optimal content display and user convenience. These files may contain information about your language preferences and previously visited pages.

Use of Personal Data by Third-Party Services

The website uses third-party services such as Google Analytics and Facebook.com, which independently collect information. The collected data may be transferred to other services within these organizations for advertising network personalization.

Links to Other Websites

AmoriaBridge may contain links to other websites that are not under our control. We are not responsible for the content of such websites. We recommend reviewing the privacy policy of each website you visit.

Changes to the Privacy Policy

AmoriaBridge may periodically update this Privacy Policy. Any changes will be published on the relevant page. We monitor changes in personal data protection legislation in the European Union and Ukraine.

Feedback and Final Provisions

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at info@amoriabridge or via the contact form on the website.Disagreement with this Privacy Policy may limit access to AmoriaBridge services.
